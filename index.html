About NIST UNIVERSITY

NIST UNIVERSITY is a reputed institution dedicated to providing quality technical education. The university provides students with a supportive learning environment, experienced faculty and modern facilities.

Our academic programs focus on both theoretical knowledge and practical skills so that students are prepared for higher education and professional careers.

Our Mission

To provide quality education and develop skilled, responsible and innovative professionals.

Our Vision

To become a leading institution recognized for excellence in education, research and innovation.
